Lemon Ricotta Gnocchi

These tender lemon ricotta gnocchi use creamy ricotta, Parmesan, and fresh lemon for a light, quick pasta-style dinner with a soft texture and bright finish.

Prep: 20 minCook: 10 minServes: 4 servings

These tender lemon ricotta gnocchi use creamy ricotta, Parmesan, and fresh lemon for a light, quick pasta-style dinner with a soft texture and bright finish. For the best result, prepare and measure the ingredients before starting. Ingredient sizes, cookware, and stove temperatures vary, so use the suggested times as a guide and check the food as it cooks. Season gradually, then taste near the end and adjust to suit your ingredients.

Why this lemon ricotta gnocchi recipe works

This recipe balances simple preparation with a few small details that make a noticeable difference. Evenly sized ingredients cook at a similar rate, and giving the pan enough room helps food brown rather than steam. Follow the order of the steps and look for the visual cues described in each one.

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin. These measured quantities make 4 servings.

  • 450 g whole-milk ricotta, well drained
  • 1 large egg
  • 70 g finely grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 120–160 g all-purpose flour, plus extra for shaping
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley or basil
  • Black pepper, to taste

Step-by-step instructions

  1. If the ricotta looks wet, place it in a fine sieve for 15–20 minutes. Drier ricotta helps the dough hold together without requiring excess flour.
  2. Mix the ricotta, egg, Parmesan, lemon zest, lemon juice, and salt in a bowl. Fold in 120 g flour, adding a little more only if the dough is too sticky to shape.
  3. Dust a work surface lightly with flour. Divide the dough into portions and roll each into a rope about 2 cm thick. Cut into bite-size pieces; keep them lightly floured.
  4. Bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil. Cook the gnocchi in batches. When they float, give them another 30–60 seconds, then lift them out with a slotted spoon.
  5. Warm the butter or oil in a wide skillet. Add the cooked gnocchi and toss gently for 1–2 minutes. Finish with herbs, black pepper, and extra Parmesan. Serve immediately.

Helpful tips

  • Prep evenly: Cut or portion ingredients to similar sizes so they cook at a consistent pace.
  • Season to taste: Salt levels differ between brands and pantry staples. Start with the listed amount and make small adjustments near the end.
  • Make it your own: Use the optional toppings and suggested swaps to suit what you have available, while keeping the main ingredient ratios close to the recipe.

Storage and make-ahead

Let leftovers cool promptly, transfer them to a clean airtight container, and refrigerate within two hours. For best quality, enjoy within 3 days and reheat only the portion you plan to eat until steaming hot. If the texture changes during storage, add a small splash of water or broth when reheating where appropriate. Do not leave cooked food at room temperature for extended periods.

What to serve with it

Serve this recipe with a simple fresh side, seasonal vegetables, or a grain that complements its flavor. Keep the rest of the meal uncomplicated so the main dish stays the focus.
