Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken

Tender, golden chicken breasts in a silky garlic cream sauce with freshly grated Parmesan and Italian herbs. This comforting skillet dinner serves eight and pairs beautifully with pasta, rice, potatoes, or vegetables.

Prep: 20 minCook: 30 minServes: 8Difficulty: Easy–moderate
🍗

Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken brings together browned chicken, fragrant garlic, cream, and Parmesan in one satisfying pan. Browning the chicken first creates flavorful bits in the skillet, which help give the sauce depth. This is an Italian-inspired comfort-food recipe rather than a claim to a traditional regional Italian dish.

Ingredients

For the chicken

  • 8 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, approximately 1.8–2 kg (4–4.4 lb) total
  • 2 teaspoons fine salt, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon paprika, optional
  • 4 tablespoons olive oil, divided

For the creamy garlic Parmesan sauce

  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 10 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups (480 ml) low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups (480 ml) heavy cream
  • 2 cups (approximately 200 g) freshly grated Parmesan cheese, loosely measured
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, or to taste
  • Salt, as needed
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley, for garnish

Ingredient notes and substitutions

  • Chicken: Choose pieces of similar size. If very thick, slice horizontally or gently pound to an even thickness.
  • Parmesan: Freshly grated cheese usually melts more smoothly than pre-grated cheese. Add it gradually over low heat.
  • Heavy cream: Whipping cream may work where it is sold under that name. Half-and-half creates a thinner sauce and can separate more easily, so keep the heat gentle.
  • Broth: Low-sodium broth makes it easier to control the salt, since Parmesan is naturally salty.
  • Gluten-free: Use a suitable gluten-free flour blend, or omit flour and simmer the sauce gently until reduced. Texture may differ.
  • Vegetables: Sauté sliced mushrooms after browning the chicken, or stir baby spinach into the sauce near the end.
  • Chicken thighs: Boneless, skinless thighs are an option; cooking time may vary. Check the thickest part with a thermometer.

Step-by-step instructions

1. Season the chicken

Pat the chicken breasts dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt, black pepper, Italian seasoning, and paprika if using. Pound or slice thick pieces to an even thickness if needed so they cook more evenly.

2. Sear the chicken

Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large, heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer, working in batches if needed. Sear for approximately 4–6 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to a clean plate; the chicken does not need to be fully cooked yet. Add the remaining oil as needed for additional batches.

Tip: Avoid crowding the pan. A crowded skillet loses heat and can steam the chicken instead of browning it.

3. Cook the garlic

Reduce the heat to medium and add the butter to the same skillet. Once melted, add the minced garlic and cook for 30–60 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant. Do not let it become dark brown, which can make the sauce bitter. Scrape up the browned bits from the skillet.

4. Make the sauce base

Sprinkle the flour over the garlic butter and stir continuously for about 1 minute. Gradually whisk in the chicken broth until smooth. Simmer for 1–2 minutes, then add the heavy cream and Italian seasoning. Warm the sauce gently; avoid a vigorous boil.

5. Melt in the Parmesan

Lower the heat and add the grated Parmesan in small handfuls, stirring after each addition until melted. Taste before adding more salt. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of broth; if it is too thin, let it simmer gently for a few minutes while stirring.

6. Finish cooking the chicken

Return the chicken and any juices on the plate to the skillet. Spoon sauce over each piece and simmer gently for approximately 5–10 minutes, depending on thickness and how much the chicken cooked during searing. Use a food thermometer to confirm the thickest part of every breast reaches 165°F (74°C).

7. Garnish and serve

Turn off the heat and garnish with chopped parsley and extra Parmesan if desired. Let the chicken rest in the sauce for 2–3 minutes, then serve with your chosen sides.

Chef tips for the best results

  • Dry the chicken first: Removing surface moisture helps the chicken brown.
  • Grate the cheese fresh: Finely grated Parmesan melts more evenly.
  • Keep the heat low after adding cheese: High heat can make a cream-and-cheese sauce grainy or separated.
  • Season at the end: Broth and Parmesan contribute salt, so taste before adding more.
  • Use a thermometer: Color alone does not confirm that chicken is safely cooked.
  • Scale your pan: For eight breasts, sear in batches and use a wide skillet or shallow pot for the final simmer.

What to serve with creamy garlic Parmesan chicken

  • Pasta: Fettuccine, linguine, or penne catches the creamy sauce.
  • Mashed potatoes or rice: Both make a simple base for the sauce.
  • Roasted vegetables: Broccoli, carrots, zucchini, or asparagus balance the rich chicken.
  • Crusty bread: Serve warm bread to scoop up extra sauce.
  • Fresh salad: A crisp salad with a lemon-based dressing adds a refreshing contrast.

Storage, reheating, and meal prep

Refrigerating leftovers

Transfer leftovers to shallow, airtight containers and refrigerate within two hours of cooking (within one hour if the ambient temperature is above 90°F/32°C). Use cooked chicken leftovers within 3–4 days.

Reheating

Reheat gently in a covered skillet over low heat, adding a small splash of broth or cream to loosen the sauce. Heat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). Reheat only the portion you plan to eat and avoid repeated reheating.

Freezing

The dish can be frozen in airtight, freezer-safe containers, but cream-based sauces may separate after thawing. Thaw in the refrigerator and reheat gently, whisking the sauce; the texture may not return completely to its original consistency.

Make-ahead preparation

Measure the seasonings, mince the garlic, grate the Parmesan, and portion the liquids ahead of time. Keep perishable ingredients refrigerated. For the best sauce texture, cook the chicken and finish the sauce close to serving time.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use chicken thighs instead of breasts?

Yes. Boneless, skinless thighs work well, but their cooking time can differ. Check the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).

Can I make the sauce without flour?

Yes. Omit the flour and simmer the sauce gently until it reduces to your preferred consistency, allowing extra time. A suitable gluten-free thickener is another option.

Why did my Parmesan sauce turn grainy?

The sauce may have become too hot or the cheese may have been added too quickly. Lower the heat, use finely grated Parmesan, and stir it in gradually.

Can I add mushrooms or spinach?

Yes. Sauté sliced mushrooms after browning the chicken. Add baby spinach near the end and stir until wilted.

Is this a traditional Italian recipe?

This is an Italian-inspired creamy chicken skillet using familiar ingredients such as garlic, Parmesan, and Italian herbs. It is not presented as a traditional dish from a specific Italian region.

Final thoughts

Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken combines golden chicken with a savory garlic cream sauce for a comforting dinner that is easy to adapt for your table. Brown the chicken in batches, melt the Parmesan over low heat, and use a thermometer to check doneness. Serve with pasta, potatoes, rice, or vegetables for a complete meal.
