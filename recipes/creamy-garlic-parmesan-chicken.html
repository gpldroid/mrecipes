Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken

Tender, golden chicken breasts in a silky garlic cream sauce with Parmesan and Italian herbs.

Prep: 20 minCook: 30 minServes: 8

Brown the chicken first, then make the sauce in the same skillet for deeper flavor. This is an Italian-inspired comfort-food recipe, not a claim of a traditional regional Italian dish.

Ingredients

For the chicken

  • 8 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, about 1.8–2 kg total
  • 2 tsp fine salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp paprika, optional
  • 4 tbsp olive oil

For the sauce

  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 10 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups (480 ml) low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups (480 ml) heavy cream
  • 2 cups (about 200 g) freshly grated Parmesan
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Salt as needed
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Pat chicken dry and season with salt, pepper, Italian seasoning and paprika.
  2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet. Sear chicken 4–6 minutes per side until golden, then reserve.
  3. Reduce heat. Melt butter and cook garlic for 30–60 seconds without browning.
  4. Stir in flour for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in broth, then add cream and Italian seasoning.
  5. Lower heat and add Parmesan in small handfuls, stirring until melted. Adjust seasoning.
  6. Return chicken and juices. Simmer gently 5–10 minutes until the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).
  7. Turn off heat, add parsley and rest 2–3 minutes before serving.

Tips

  • Dry the chicken well before searing.
  • Use freshly grated Parmesan and add it over low heat.
  • Avoid vigorous boiling after adding cream and cheese.
  • Add spinach or mushrooms if desired.

Serving suggestions

Serve with pasta, rice, mashed potatoes, roasted vegetables or crusty bread. A fresh lemon-dressed salad balances the rich sauce.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate leftovers promptly in an airtight container and use cooked chicken within 3–4 days. Reheat gently with a splash of broth or cream.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use chicken thighs?

Yes. Cooking time varies with thickness; check for 165°F (74°C) internally.

Can I make the sauce without flour?

Yes. Simmer gently until reduced to the desired consistency or use a suitable gluten-free thickener.

Why is the sauce grainy?

High heat or adding cheese too quickly can cause separation. Keep the heat low and add Parmesan gradually.

Recipe notes

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Adjust seasoning to taste and follow reliable food-safety guidance.
