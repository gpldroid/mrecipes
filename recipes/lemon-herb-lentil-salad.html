Lemon Herb Lentil Salad

Toss tender lentils with cucumber, tomatoes, parsley, and a bright lemon-Dijon dressing for a fresh make-ahead salad.

Prep: 15 minCook: 20 minYield: 4 servings

At a glance: 35 minutes · 4 servings · Salad.

This lentil salad is hearty enough for lunch but brightened with lemon, herbs, and crunchy vegetables. Use green or brown lentils that hold their shape after cooking rather than red lentils, which soften into a purée.

Lentils absorb flavor while still warm, so tossing them with part of the dressing soon after cooking gives the salad a more balanced taste. Fresh herbs and crisp vegetables go in after the lentils cool slightly.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 200 g dried green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 small bay leaf, optional
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt for cooking water, plus to taste
  • 1/2 cucumber, diced
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 3 spring onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 50 g crumbled feta, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Put the rinsed lentils in a saucepan and cover with plenty of water. Add the bay leaf if using and bring to a boil.
  2. Reduce to a gentle simmer and cook for 18–25 minutes, checking near the end, until tender but still holding their shape. Cooking time depends on the age and variety of the lentils.
  3. Drain well, remove the bay leaf, and season lightly with salt while the lentils are still warm. Let steam-dry for a few minutes.
  4. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, garlic, black pepper, and a pinch of salt in a large bowl.
  5. Add the warm lentils and toss with about half the dressing. Let cool until just warm, then add cucumber, tomatoes, spring onions, and parsley.
  6. Toss with the remaining dressing, taste and adjust salt or lemon, then add feta if desired. Serve slightly warm or chilled.

Tips for the best results

  • Avoid overcooking the lentils; begin checking early and drain as soon as they are tender.
  • If the salad tastes flat, add a little lemon juice or salt before adding more oil.
  • For a more substantial meal, serve with toasted pita or add roasted vegetables.

Serving suggestions

Serve as a side with grilled vegetables or as a light lunch with warm flatbread. Feta adds a salty, creamy contrast.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The lentils absorb dressing over time, so refresh with a squeeze of lemon and a drizzle of olive oil before serving.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use canned lentils?

Yes. Drain and rinse them well, then skip the simmering step. Fold them gently into the dressing to keep them intact.

Which lentils are best for salad?

Green or brown lentils usually keep their shape. Red lentils soften much more and are better suited to soups and dals.
