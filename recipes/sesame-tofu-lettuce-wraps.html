Sesame Tofu Lettuce Wraps

Crisp lettuce cups filled with golden tofu, ginger, garlic, and a savory sesame sauce make a fresh, customizable plant-based meal ready in about 30 minutes.

Prep: 15 minCook: 15 minServes: 4 servings

Why this sesame tofu lettuce wraps recipe works

This recipe balances simple preparation with a few small details that make a noticeable difference. Evenly sized ingredients cook at a similar rate, and giving the pan enough room helps food brown rather than steam. Follow the order of the steps and look for the visual cues described in each one.

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin. These measured quantities make 4 servings.

  • 400 g firm tofu, pressed and crumbled
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 small carrot, finely diced
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 1 head butter lettuce, leaves separated
  • Sesame seeds and lime wedges, to serve

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Pat the tofu dry and crumble it into small pieces. Separate and wash the lettuce leaves, then dry them well so the filling stays in place.
  2. Whisk the soy sauce, rice vinegar, hoisin, and sesame oil in a small bowl. Set aside.
  3. Heat the neutral oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu in one layer and cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly golden and most moisture has evaporated.
  4. Add the garlic, ginger, carrot, and half the scallions. Cook for 2 minutes, then pour in the sauce and stir until the tofu is evenly coated and glossy.
  5. Spoon the warm filling into lettuce leaves. Top with the remaining scallions and sesame seeds, and serve with lime wedges. Keep extra filling separate if preparing ahead.

Helpful tips

  • Prep evenly: Cut or portion ingredients to similar sizes so they cook at a consistent pace.
  • Season to taste: Salt levels differ between brands and pantry staples. Start with the listed amount and make small adjustments near the end.
  • Make it your own: Use the optional toppings and suggested swaps to suit what you have available, while keeping the main ingredient ratios close to the recipe.

Storage and make-ahead

Let leftovers cool promptly, transfer them to a clean airtight container, and refrigerate within two hours. For best quality, enjoy within 3 days and reheat only the portion you plan to eat until steaming hot. If the texture changes during storage, add a small splash of water or broth when reheating where appropriate. Do not leave cooked food at room temperature for extended periods.

What to serve with it

Serve this recipe with a simple fresh side, seasonal vegetables, or a grain that complements its flavor. Keep the rest of the meal uncomplicated so the main dish stays the focus.
