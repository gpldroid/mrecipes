Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Bake chocolate chip cookies with golden edges, soft centers and plenty of chocolate using this approachable homemade recipe.

Prep: 15 minCook: 12 minServes: 18

This simple dough gives golden edges and soft centers. Remove the cookies while the centers still look slightly underdone; they set as they cool.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Beat butter and both sugars until creamy, about 2 minutes.
  3. Beat in egg and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Whisk flour, baking soda and salt separately. Add and mix just until combined.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips. Scoop rounded tablespoons onto the sheet, leaving about 2 inches between cookies.
  6. Bake 9–12 minutes until edges are lightly golden and centers look soft.
  7. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

Tips

  • Chill dough for 30 minutes for thicker cookies.
  • Measure flour by weight for consistency.
  • Do not overbake the centers.

Storage and serving

Store cooled cookies in an airtight container. Dough and baked cookies can also be frozen in suitable freezer packaging.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use chopped chocolate?

Yes. The pieces will simply vary more in size.

Why do my cookies spread too much?

The dough may be too warm or the butter too liquid. Chilling the dough helps.

Recipe notes

Baking time varies with oven and cookie size. Watch the edges and center as well as the clock.
