Juicy Baked Chicken Breast

Bake seasoned boneless chicken breasts until juicy and tender with garlic, paprika, olive oil, and clear temperature-based doneness guidance.

Prep: 10 minCook: 20 minYield: 4 servings

At a glance: 30 minutes · 4 servings · Chicken Dinner.

Chicken breast can dry out quickly because it is lean. This straightforward oven method uses evenly sized pieces, a little oil, and a thermometer to help you stop cooking as soon as the thickest part is safely done.

Even thickness helps each piece cook at a similar rate. Oil encourages browning and carries seasoning, while a short rest allows juices to settle before slicing.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, about 680–800 g total
  • 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 3/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, to finish
  • Chopped parsley, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C / 425°F. Lightly oil a baking dish or line a rimmed sheet pan.
  2. Pat chicken breasts dry. If one end is much thicker, gently pound the thickest part to an even thickness without tearing the meat.
  3. Mix olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and oregano. Rub the mixture over all sides of the chicken.
  4. Arrange chicken in a single layer with a little space between pieces. Bake for 16–22 minutes, depending on thickness.
  5. Check the thickest part of each breast with an instant-read thermometer. Poultry should reach 74°C / 165°F; return any under-temperature pieces to the oven and recheck.
  6. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes, then finish with lemon juice and parsley. Slice across the grain and serve.

Tips for the best results

  • Use temperature, not color alone, to determine doneness; pieces of different sizes may finish at different times.
  • If the breasts are very large, slice horizontally into cutlets or pound them evenly to shorten cooking time.
  • Resting is useful, but do not rely on carryover cooking to bring undercooked poultry to a safe temperature.

Serving suggestions

Slice across the grain and serve with roasted vegetables, rice, or a crisp salad. Spoon any pan juices over the chicken.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Refrigerate cooked chicken within 2 hours in a shallow, covered container and use within 3–4 days. Reheat until steaming hot; freeze portions for up to 2 months for best quality.

Frequently asked questions

How do I stop chicken breast drying out?

Even out thickness, avoid overcooking, and verify the center reaches 74°C / 165°F with a thermometer.

Can I use frozen chicken breasts?

Thaw completely in the refrigerator first for more even seasoning and predictable cooking.
