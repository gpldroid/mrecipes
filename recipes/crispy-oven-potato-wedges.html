Crispy Oven Potato Wedges

Make golden oven potato wedges with fluffy centers, paprika and garlic seasoning, and crisp edges using a hot tray and simple pantry spices.

Prep: 12 minCook: 35 minYield: 4 servings

At a glance: 47 minutes · 4 servings · Side Dish.

These potato wedges are cut thick enough to stay fluffy inside but have plenty of surface area for browning. A hot oven, dry potatoes, and room on the baking tray help them roast rather than steam.

Rinsing away excess surface starch and drying the wedges well helps the edges crisp. Turning them once exposes more sides to the hot tray, while a little oil carries the seasoning across the surface.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 900 g russet or floury potatoes, scrubbed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 3/4 tsp fine salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C / 425°F. Place a sturdy rimmed baking sheet in the oven while it heats.
  2. Cut each potato lengthwise into halves, then cut each half into 3–4 even wedges. Rinse briefly in cold water if time allows, then dry very thoroughly with a clean towel.
  3. Toss the wedges in a bowl with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
  4. Carefully spread wedges on the hot tray with a cut side down, leaving space between pieces. Use two trays if needed to avoid crowding.
  5. Roast for 20 minutes, turn the wedges, and roast another 12–18 minutes until deeply golden and tender when pierced with a fork.
  6. Taste one wedge and add a little more salt if needed. Finish with parsley and serve hot.

Tips for the best results

  • Dry the wedges thoroughly after rinsing; surface water slows browning.
  • Avoid lining the tray with a thick silicone mat if maximum crispness is the goal.
  • Cut wedges to a similar thickness so the smaller pieces do not burn before the larger ones soften.

Serving suggestions

Serve straight from the oven with a yogurt-herb dip, ketchup, or alongside grilled vegetables and a main dish.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Refrigerate leftovers in a covered container for up to 3 days. Re-crisp on a baking sheet at 200°C / 400°F for 8–12 minutes; microwaving softens the edges.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to peel the potatoes?

No. Scrub the skins well and leave them on for texture, or peel if you prefer.

Why are my wedges soft?

The tray may be crowded, the potatoes may not have been dried, or the oven may not have been fully preheated.
