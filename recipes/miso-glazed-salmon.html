Miso-Glazed Salmon

Make miso-glazed salmon with a savory-sweet glaze, tender flakes, and lightly caramelized edges. This quick oven recipe pairs well with rice and crisp vegetables.

Prep: 10 minCook: 15 minServes: 4 servings

Make miso-glazed salmon with a savory-sweet glaze, tender flakes, and lightly caramelized edges. This quick oven recipe pairs well with rice and crisp vegetables. Why this miso-glazed salmon recipe works

This recipe balances simple preparation with a few small details that make a noticeable difference. Evenly sized ingredients cook at a similar rate, and giving the pan enough room helps food brown rather than steam. Follow the order of the steps and look for the visual cues described in each one.

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin. These measured quantities make 4 servings.

  • 4 salmon fillets, about 150–170 g each
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp finely grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp neutral oil
  • Sliced scallions and sesame seeds, to serve (optional)

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C / 425°F. Line a rimmed baking tray with baking paper and lightly brush it with oil.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk the miso, honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and ginger until smooth. If the paste is very thick, loosen it with a teaspoon of water.
  3. Pat the salmon dry and place it skin-side down on the tray. Spread the glaze evenly over the tops, keeping it off the tray where it could burn.
  4. Bake for 8–12 minutes, depending on thickness, until the salmon flakes easily and reaches 63°C / 145°F at the thickest point. For a more caramelized top, briefly use the broiler while watching closely.
  5. Rest for 2 minutes, then finish with scallions or sesame seeds if desired. Serve with rice and steamed greens.

Helpful tips

  • Prep evenly: Cut or portion ingredients to similar sizes so they cook at a consistent pace.
  • Season to taste: Salt levels differ between brands and pantry staples. Start with the listed amount and make small adjustments near the end.
  • Make it your own: Use the optional toppings and suggested swaps to suit what you have available, while keeping the main ingredient ratios close to the recipe.

Storage and make-ahead

Let leftovers cool promptly, transfer them to a clean airtight container, and refrigerate within two hours. For best quality, enjoy within 3 days and reheat only the portion you plan to eat until steaming hot. If the texture changes during storage, add a small splash of water or broth when reheating where appropriate. Do not leave cooked food at room temperature for extended periods.

What to serve with it

Serve this recipe with a simple fresh side, seasonal vegetables, or a grain that complements its flavor. Keep the rest of the meal uncomplicated so the main dish stays the focus.
