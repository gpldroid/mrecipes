Coconut Lime Rice Bowl

Coconut lime rice bowls pair fluffy coconut-scented rice with black beans, crisp vegetables, and a bright lime dressing for a flexible, satisfying plant-based meal.

Prep: 15 minCook: 20 minServes: 4 servings

Recipe at a glance: 35 minutes · 4 servings · Lunch · Primary keyword: coconut lime rice bowl.

Coconut lime rice bowls pair fluffy coconut-scented rice with black beans, crisp vegetables, and a bright lime dressing for a flexible, satisfying plant-based meal. The method below keeps the steps straightforward and gives you clear cues for texture and doneness, so you can cook with confidence instead of relying on the timer alone.

For the best result, prepare and measure the ingredients before starting. Ingredient sizes, cookware, and stove temperatures vary, so use the suggested times as a guide and check the food as it cooks. Season gradually, then taste near the end and adjust to suit your ingredients.

Why this coconut lime rice bowl recipe works

This recipe balances simple preparation with a few small details that make a noticeable difference. Evenly sized ingredients cook at a similar rate, and giving the pan enough room helps food brown rather than steam. Follow the order of the steps and look for the visual cues described in each one.

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin. These measured quantities make 4 servings.

  • 250 g long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 200 ml light coconut milk
  • 300 ml water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 can black beans, 400 g, drained and rinsed
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 lime, zest and juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Black pepper, to taste

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Combine the rinsed rice, coconut milk, water, and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a gentle boil, then cover and reduce the heat to low.
  2. Cook for 15–18 minutes, or according to the rice package, until the liquid is absorbed. Remove from the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  3. While the rice cooks, warm the black beans and corn in a small pan with the olive oil and a pinch of pepper. Add a splash of water if they begin to stick.
  4. Mix the lime juice, lime zest, and cilantro in a small bowl. Taste and add a little water if you prefer a milder dressing.
  5. Divide the coconut rice among bowls. Add beans, corn, bell pepper, and avocado, then drizzle with lime dressing. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Helpful tips

  • Prep evenly: Cut or portion ingredients to similar sizes so they cook at a consistent pace.
  • Season to taste: Salt levels differ between brands and pantry staples. Start with the listed amount and make small adjustments near the end.
  • Make it your own: Use the optional toppings and suggested swaps to suit what you have available, while keeping the main ingredient ratios close to the recipe.

Storage and make-ahead

Let leftovers cool promptly, transfer them to a clean airtight container, and refrigerate within two hours. For best quality, enjoy within 3 days and reheat only the portion you plan to eat until steaming hot. If the texture changes during storage, add a small splash of water or broth when reheating where appropriate. Do not leave cooked food at room temperature for extended periods.

What to serve with it

Serve this recipe with a simple fresh side, seasonal vegetables, or a grain that complements its flavor. Keep the rest of the meal uncomplicated so the main dish stays the focus.
