Easy Coconut Chickpea Curry

Simmer chickpeas in a fragrant tomato and coconut curry with ginger, garlic, and warming spices for a satisfying plant-based dinner.

Prep: 10 minCook: 25 minYield: 4 servings

At a glance: 35 minutes · 4 servings · Vegetarian Dinner.

This chickpea curry makes a pantry-friendly dinner from canned chickpeas, tomatoes, and coconut milk. The sauce is gently spiced rather than fiery, so you can adjust the heat at the table.

Cooking the spices briefly in oil helps their aroma bloom before liquid is added. Tomatoes provide acidity, while coconut milk softens the spices and creates a silky sauce that clings to the chickpeas.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil or olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp freshly grated ginger
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp chili flakes, optional
  • 1 can (400 g / 14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (400 ml / 13.5 oz) coconut milk
  • 2 cans (about 480 g drained total) chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt, then adjust to taste
  • 2 cups baby spinach, optional
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Chopped cilantro, to serve

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Warm the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5–7 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
  2. Stir in garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds, just until fragrant.
  3. Add curry powder, cumin, and optional chili flakes. Stir for 20–30 seconds so the spices coat the onion without scorching.
  4. Pour in the tomatoes and coconut milk. Stir, scraping up anything on the base, then bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Add the rinsed chickpeas and salt. Simmer uncovered for 15–18 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and the chickpeas are hot throughout.
  6. Stir in spinach, if using, until wilted. Finish with lime juice, taste, and adjust salt or chili.
  7. Serve with rice or flatbread and chopped cilantro.

Tips for the best results

  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer rather than a hard boil to reduce splattering and preserve a smooth texture.
  • Mash a few chickpeas against the side of the pan if you want a thicker sauce without adding flour.
  • Add lime juice at the end to keep its fresh flavor bright.

Serving suggestions

Serve over basmati rice or with warm flatbread, plus extra lime and cilantro at the table.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Cool promptly and refrigerate in a covered container for up to 3–4 days. Reheat gently on the stove with a splash of water. The curry freezes well for up to 2 months; thaw overnight in the refrigerator.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make this curry without coconut milk?

Use vegetable broth and a few spoonfuls of plain yogurt stirred in off the heat, or use a different tested creamy base. The flavor and texture will change.

Is canned chickpea liquid needed?

No. Drain and rinse the chickpeas for a cleaner sauce; add a splash of water or broth only if the curry becomes too thick.
