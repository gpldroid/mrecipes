Warm Roasted Vegetable Salad

A colorful salad of caramelized vegetables tossed with a bright lemon dressing. Serve warm as a light lunch or as a side for grilled mains.

Prep: 15 minCook: 25 minServes: 4

A colorful salad of caramelized vegetables tossed with a bright lemon dressing. Serve warm as a light lunch or as a side for grilled mains.

This approachable recipe uses everyday ingredients and clear steps to help you prepare a flavorful homemade meal. Read through the method before starting, prep the ingredients, and adjust seasoning to suit your taste.

Why you’ll love this recipe

It is practical for a home kitchen, easy to serve, and flexible enough for simple ingredient swaps. For the best result, prepare the ingredients before cooking and taste the dish before serving.

Ingredients

Gather and measure the following ingredients before you begin.

  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 small red onion, cut into wedges
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 cups mixed salad greens
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F (220°C). Place pepper, zucchini, onion, and tomatoes on a large baking tray.
  2. Toss vegetables with olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Spread in one layer and roast for 20–25 minutes, turning once, until tender and lightly browned.
  3. Whisk lemon juice, Dijon mustard, and extra-virgin olive oil in a small bowl. Season to taste.
  4. Arrange greens on a platter and add the warm roasted vegetables. Drizzle with dressing and toss gently.
  5. Finish with feta if desired and serve straight away while the vegetables are warm.

Tips for the best results

  • Cut ingredients into similar sizes so they cook evenly.
  • Season in stages and taste before serving; ingredient brands and salt levels vary.
  • Use a food thermometer for meat and poultry rather than relying on color alone.
  • For substitutions, expect that texture and cooking time may change slightly.

Serving ideas

Serve freshly prepared while the texture and flavor are at their best. Add a simple green salad, seasonal vegetables, or bread where appropriate to round out the meal.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate perishable leftovers promptly in a clean, covered container. As a general food-safety guideline, use cooked leftovers within 3–4 days and reheat thoroughly. Keep sauces and salads separate when that helps preserve texture.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare this recipe ahead?

You can measure and prep many ingredients in advance. For best texture, cook delicate components close to serving time and store raw ingredients safely in the refrigerator.

How do I adjust the seasoning?

Start with the listed amounts, taste near the end, then add salt, pepper, herbs, or acidity a little at a time.

Recipe notes

Cooking times are estimates and may vary with equipment, ingredient size, and kitchen conditions. Follow reliable food-safety guidance for meat, eggs, and other perishable ingredients.
