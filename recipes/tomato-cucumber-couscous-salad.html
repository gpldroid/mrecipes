Lemon Tomato Cucumber Couscous Salad

Make a bright couscous salad with tomatoes, cucumber, parsley, lemon, and olive oil for a quick side dish or light lunch.

Prep: 15 minCook: 5 minYield: 4 servings

At a glance: 20 minutes · 4 servings · Salad.

This couscous salad is a quick alternative to a grain salad that needs a long simmer. Fine couscous steams in minutes, then cools before it is tossed with juicy tomatoes, cucumber, parsley, and lemon dressing.

Fluffing couscous with a fork separates the grains, while adding dressing after it cools keeps the herbs fresh and the vegetables crisp. A short rest lets the lemon and olive oil season the grains evenly.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 200 g fine couscous
  • 240 ml boiling vegetable stock or water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, for couscous
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt, adjust if using stock
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cucumber, diced
  • 3 spring onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup chopped parsley
  • 2 tbsp chopped mint, optional
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Black pepper, to taste

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Place couscous in a heatproof bowl with the first tablespoon of olive oil and salt. Pour over boiling stock or water and stir once.
  2. Cover the bowl and leave for 5 minutes, or follow the package directions if they differ.
  3. Uncover and fluff the couscous with a fork, breaking up any clumps. Spread it on a tray or large plate to cool more quickly.
  4. Whisk extra-virgin olive oil, lemon juice, and black pepper in a large serving bowl.
  5. Add the cooled couscous, tomatoes, cucumber, spring onions, parsley, and optional mint. Toss until evenly mixed.
  6. Taste and adjust with extra lemon juice, salt, or pepper. Let stand for 5–10 minutes before serving so the flavors combine.

Tips for the best results

  • Let the couscous cool before adding herbs to keep them bright and fragrant.
  • If using salted stock, wait until the salad is mixed before adding more salt.
  • Dice the vegetables fairly small so each forkful includes a little of everything.

Serving suggestions

Serve chilled or at cool room temperature as a side for roasted vegetables, grilled protein, or a picnic-style meal.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 3 days. The couscous absorbs dressing as it sits, so refresh with lemon juice or a little olive oil before serving. Keep at room temperature only for the time needed to serve.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make couscous salad ahead?

Yes. Prepare it several hours ahead and refrigerate; add a little lemon juice and fresh herbs before serving if needed.

Can I use pearl couscous?

Pearl couscous requires a different cooking method and more time. Cook it according to its package, drain, cool, and then use the same salad mix-ins.
