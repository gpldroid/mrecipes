Apple Cinnamon Crumble Bars

Apple cinnamon crumble bars layer soft spiced apples between a buttery oat crust and golden crumb topping. They slice neatly once cooled and travel well for sharing.

Prep: 20 minCook: 40 minServes: 12 bars

Apple cinnamon crumble bars layer soft spiced apples between a buttery oat crust and golden crumb topping. They slice neatly once cooled and travel well for sharing. Why this apple cinnamon crumble bars recipe works

This recipe balances simple preparation with a few small details that make a noticeable difference. Evenly sized ingredients cook at a similar rate, and giving the pan enough room helps food brown rather than steam. Follow the order of the steps and look for the visual cues described in each one.

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin. These measured quantities make 12 bars.

  • 180 g all-purpose flour
  • 100 g rolled oats
  • 120 g light brown sugar, divided
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon, plus 1 tsp for apples
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 140 g cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 2 medium apples, peeled and diced small
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C / 350°F. Line a 20 cm / 8-inch square pan with baking paper, leaving overhang on two sides.
  2. Mix the flour, oats, 80 g brown sugar, baking powder, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, and salt. Rub in the cold butter until the mixture forms damp, uneven crumbs.
  3. Press about two-thirds of the crumb mixture firmly into the pan. Bake for 12 minutes, until the base looks set but not deeply browned.
  4. Toss the diced apples with lemon juice, remaining 40 g sugar, cinnamon, cornstarch, and vanilla. Spread the apples evenly over the warm base, then scatter the remaining crumbs on top.
  5. Bake for 28–35 minutes until the topping is golden and the apple layer bubbles at the edges. Cool completely in the pan before lifting out and cutting into 12 bars.

Helpful tips

  • Prep evenly: Cut or portion ingredients to similar sizes so they cook at a consistent pace.
  • Season to taste: Salt levels differ between brands and pantry staples. Start with the listed amount and make small adjustments near the end.
  • Make it your own: Use the optional toppings and suggested swaps to suit what you have available, while keeping the main ingredient ratios close to the recipe.

Storage and make-ahead

Let leftovers cool promptly, transfer them to a clean airtight container, and refrigerate within two hours. For best quality, enjoy within 3 days and reheat only the portion you plan to eat until steaming hot. If the texture changes during storage, add a small splash of water or broth when reheating where appropriate. Do not leave cooked food at room temperature for extended periods.

What to serve with it

Serve this recipe with a simple fresh side, seasonal vegetables, or a grain that complements its flavor. Keep the rest of the meal uncomplicated so the main dish stays the focus.
