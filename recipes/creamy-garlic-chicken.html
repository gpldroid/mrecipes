Creamy Garlic Chicken

Tender pan-seared chicken with fresh garlic and a silky Parmesan cream sauce. An easy weeknight dinner ready in about 30 minutes.

Prep: 10 minCook: 20 minServes: 4

Creamy garlic chicken combines browned chicken, fresh garlic and Parmesan in one comforting skillet. It is quick enough for a weeknight while still feeling special.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3/4 cup chicken broth
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Pat chicken dry and season with salt, pepper and paprika.
  2. Heat olive oil over medium-high heat. Sear chicken 5–7 minutes per side until golden and cooked through; transfer to a plate.
  3. Reduce heat to medium and cook garlic for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add broth and scrape up browned bits. Simmer 2 minutes, then stir in cream, Parmesan and Italian seasoning.
  5. Simmer gently 3–5 minutes until slightly thickened. Return chicken and juices to the skillet and spoon sauce over it.
  6. Finish with parsley and serve with rice, pasta or vegetables.

Tips for the best results

  • Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).
  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer after adding cream.
  • Half-and-half can be used for a lighter sauce.

Storage and serving

Refrigerate leftovers promptly in a covered container and generally use cooked leftovers within 3–4 days. Reheat thoroughly and gently.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use chicken thighs?

Yes. Cooking time can vary with thickness, so check the internal temperature.

Can I omit Parmesan?

Yes, but the sauce will be less rich. Add a little broth to adjust the consistency.

Recipe notes

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Adjust seasoning to taste and follow reliable food-safety guidance.
