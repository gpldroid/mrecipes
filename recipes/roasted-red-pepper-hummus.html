Roasted Red Pepper Hummus

Roasted red pepper hummus blends chickpeas, tahini, lemon, garlic, and sweet roasted peppers into a smooth dip for vegetables, pita, and lunch bowls.

Roasted red pepper hummus blends chickpeas, tahini, lemon, garlic, and sweet roasted peppers into a smooth dip for vegetables, pita, and lunch bowls. Why this roasted red pepper hummus recipe works

This recipe balances simple preparation with a few small details that make a noticeable difference. Evenly sized ingredients cook at a similar rate, and giving the pan enough room helps food brown rather than steam. Follow the order of the steps and look for the visual cues described in each one.

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin. These measured quantities make 8 servings.

  • 1 can chickpeas, 400 g, drained and rinsed
  • 1 large roasted red pepper, skin removed and drained
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice, plus more to taste
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus extra to serve
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2–4 tbsp cold water
  • Salt, to taste
  • Paprika and chopped parsley, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Add the chickpeas, roasted pepper, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, cumin, and a pinch of salt to a food processor.
  2. Blend for 30 seconds, scrape down the sides, and blend again until the mixture begins to look smooth.
  3. With the processor running, add cold water one tablespoon at a time until the hummus reaches your preferred creamy consistency.
  4. Taste and adjust with more lemon juice or salt. Blend once more to distribute the seasoning evenly.
  5. Transfer to a bowl, swirl the surface with the back of a spoon, and finish with a little olive oil, paprika, or parsley. Serve with pita or crunchy vegetables.

Helpful tips

  • Prep evenly: Cut or portion ingredients to similar sizes so they cook at a consistent pace.
  • Season to taste: Salt levels differ between brands and pantry staples. Start with the listed amount and make small adjustments near the end.
  • Make it your own: Use the optional toppings and suggested swaps to suit what you have available, while keeping the main ingredient ratios close to the recipe.

Storage and make-ahead

Let leftovers cool promptly, transfer them to a clean airtight container, and refrigerate within two hours. For best quality, enjoy within 3 days and reheat only the portion you plan to eat until steaming hot. If the texture changes during storage, add a small splash of water or broth when reheating where appropriate. Do not leave cooked food at room temperature for extended periods.

What to serve with it

Serve this recipe with a simple fresh side, seasonal vegetables, or a grain that complements its flavor. Keep the rest of the meal uncomplicated so the main dish stays the focus.
