15-Minute Garlic Butter Pasta

Toss pasta with fragrant garlic butter, Parmesan, black pepper, and starchy pasta water for a fast, glossy weeknight meal.

Prep: 5 minCook: 10 minYield: 2 servings

At a glance: 15 minutes · 2 servings · Pasta Dinner.

Garlic butter pasta is simple, but the details matter: gently cook the garlic so it stays sweet, reserve pasta water, and toss the cheese off the strongest heat. The result is a light glossy coating rather than a pool of butter at the bottom.

Starchy pasta water helps butter and Parmesan emulsify into a sauce that clings to the noodles. Slowly warming sliced garlic in butter infuses the fat without browning it too quickly.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 200 g spaghetti or linguine
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes, optional
  • 30 g finely grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt for pasta water, plus to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1/2 lemon, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil, salt it, and cook pasta according to the package until just al dente. Before draining, reserve at least 180 ml pasta water.
  2. While the pasta cooks, melt butter with olive oil in a wide skillet over low heat.
  3. Add sliced garlic and optional pepper flakes. Cook gently for 1–2 minutes, stirring often, until fragrant but not browned.
  4. Transfer the drained pasta to the skillet with 60 ml reserved pasta water. Toss over low heat until the noodles are coated.
  5. Remove the skillet from the heat. Add Parmesan and black pepper, tossing continuously. Add more pasta water a tablespoon at a time until the sauce looks glossy.
  6. Stir in parsley and optional lemon zest or a small squeeze of juice. Taste and adjust seasoning, then serve with extra Parmesan.

Tips for the best results

  • Keep the garlic pale; browned garlic can taste bitter and dominate the sauce.
  • Finely grate the Parmesan so it melts quickly and evenly.
  • Add cheese off the heat to reduce clumping, and use pasta water to loosen the sauce rather than adding more butter immediately.

Serving suggestions

Serve immediately with a green salad or steamed vegetables. The pasta is delicate, so keep sides simple and fresh.

Storage and make-ahead notes

This pasta is best freshly made. Refrigerate leftovers promptly for up to 3 days and reheat gently in a skillet with a splash of water; the sauce may be less glossy after chilling.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make garlic butter pasta without Parmesan?

Yes. Skip it and use a little more pasta water to help the butter coat the noodles, then season carefully.

What if the sauce looks oily?

Take the pan off the heat and toss in a small splash of warm pasta water until the butter disperses and coats the noodles.
