Creamy Overnight Oats

Prepare creamy overnight oats with rolled oats, milk, yogurt, and chia seeds for a make-ahead breakfast that is easy to customize.

Prep: 10 minCook: 0 minYield: 2 servings

At a glance: 10 minutes · 2 servings · Breakfast.

Overnight oats soften in the refrigerator instead of on the stove, making breakfast easy to prepare ahead. This version uses yogurt for body and chia seeds to help the mixture thicken without cooking.

Rolled oats absorb liquid slowly overnight, while yogurt adds tang and chia seeds bind excess moisture. Stirring once before serving distributes the thickened mixture evenly.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 100 g old-fashioned rolled oats (about 1 cup)
  • 240 ml milk of choice (1 cup)
  • 120 g plain yogurt (1/2 cup)
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey, optional
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of fine salt
  • 1/2 cup berries or sliced banana, for serving
  • 1 tbsp chopped nuts or seeds, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Divide the oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, sweetener if using, vanilla, and salt between two clean jars or lidded containers.
  2. Stir thoroughly, scraping the bottom so no dry chia seeds or oats remain stuck to the sides.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight, until the oats are soft and the mixture has thickened.
  4. In the morning, stir again. Add a splash of milk if you prefer a looser consistency.
  5. Top with fresh fruit and nuts just before serving.

Tips for the best results

  • Use old-fashioned rolled oats for a creamy texture; steel-cut oats need a different liquid ratio and longer soaking.
  • Add crunchy toppings just before eating so they stay crisp.
  • For a dairy-free version, choose plant-based milk and yogurt with similar thickness.

Serving suggestions

Pair the oats with fresh fruit, toasted nuts, or a little extra yogurt. For meal prep, keep crunchy toppings in a separate container until breakfast.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Keep covered in the refrigerator and eat within 3 days. Add cut fruit close to serving time for the best texture. Do not leave the prepared oats at room temperature for extended periods.

Frequently asked questions

Can I heat overnight oats?

Yes. Transfer a portion to a microwave-safe bowl and warm gently, stirring and adding milk as needed.

Why are my oats too thick?

Chia seeds and oats absorb different amounts of liquid. Stir in milk a tablespoon at a time until the texture is right.
