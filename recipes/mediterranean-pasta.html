Easy Mediterranean Pasta

A quick Mediterranean pasta with juicy tomatoes, briny olives, garlic, lemon and fresh herbs. Bright, satisfying and easy to adapt.

Prep: 10 minCook: 15 minServes: 4

Tomatoes, olives, garlic, lemon and herbs create a bright sauce. Reserved pasta water helps the sauce cling to the noodles.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) pasta such as penne or fusilli
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup pitted Kalamata olives
  • 1/3 cup crumbled feta
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Zest and juice of 1/2 lemon
  • Fresh parsley or basil
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water and drain.
  2. Warm olive oil over medium heat and cook garlic for 30 seconds.
  3. Add tomatoes, olives, oregano and pepper. Cook 5–7 minutes.
  4. Add pasta, lemon and a splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy.
  5. Remove from heat. Fold in feta and herbs; taste before salting.
  6. Serve immediately with extra herbs if desired.

Tips

  • Add baby spinach with the tomatoes.
  • Add chickpeas or cooked chicken for more protein.
  • Taste before salting because feta and olives are salty.

Storage and serving

This pasta is best fresh. Refrigerate leftovers promptly for 3–4 days and reheat gently with a splash of water.

Frequently asked questions

Can I replace feta?

Yes. Use another crumbly mild cheese and adjust salt.

Can I add chicken?

Yes. Add cooked chopped chicken at the end to warm it through.

Recipe notes

Cooking times vary with pasta type and equipment. Adjust seasoning to taste.
