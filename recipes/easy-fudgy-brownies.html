Easy Fudgy Brownies

Make deeply chocolatey fudgy brownies with a shiny top, chewy edges, and a soft center using one bowl and everyday baking ingredients.

Prep: 15 minCook: 25 minYield: 16 brownies

At a glance: 40 minutes · 16 brownies · Dessert.

These easy fudgy brownies are built around melted chocolate and butter rather than a cake-like cocoa batter, giving the center a dense, soft bite. A brief rest before cutting helps the squares set cleanly.

The combination of melted chocolate, a modest amount of flour, and just enough mixing keeps the crumb dense. Dissolving the sugar into the warm butter-chocolate mixture also helps create a delicate glossy crust.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 170 g dark chocolate, roughly chopped
  • 115 g unsalted butter
  • 200 g granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 75 g all-purpose flour
  • 25 g unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 80 g chocolate chips or chopped chocolate, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F. Line an 20 cm / 8-inch square pan with parchment, leaving a little overhang to lift the brownies out later.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate and butter together in short microwave bursts or over a pan of barely simmering water. Stir until smooth and let cool for 3–5 minutes.
  3. Whisk the sugar into the warm chocolate mixture. Add the eggs one at a time, whisking until glossy, then mix in the vanilla.
  4. Sift in the flour, cocoa, and salt. Fold just until no dry streaks remain; avoid beating the batter once the flour is added.
  5. Fold in the optional chocolate chips and spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan.
  6. Bake for 22–27 minutes. The edges should look set and a toothpick inserted near the center should come out with moist crumbs, not wet batter.
  7. Cool completely in the pan before lifting out and slicing into 16 squares with a clean knife.

Tips for the best results

  • For a fudgy center, stop baking when moist crumbs cling to the tester; a clean, dry tester usually means a firmer brownie.
  • Use chocolate you enjoy eating, ideally around 55–70% cocoa.
  • For neat slices, chill the cooled slab for 30 minutes and wipe the knife between cuts.

Serving suggestions

Serve the brownies on their own or with berries and a spoonful of yogurt or ice cream. Cut smaller squares for a dessert platter.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Keep brownies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days. Freeze individually wrapped squares for up to 2 months and thaw before serving.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use only cocoa powder?

This formula is designed for melted chocolate. Replacing it with cocoa changes the fat and sugar balance, so use a tested cocoa-only brownie formula instead.

How do I know when brownies are done?

The perimeter should be set while the center remains slightly soft. A tester should show damp crumbs rather than liquid batter.
