Mushroom Barley Soup

Mushroom barley soup combines browned mushrooms, vegetables, herbs, and chewy pearl barley in a deeply savory broth for a comforting one-pot lunch or dinner.

Prep: 15 minCook: 45 minServes: 6 servings

Mushroom barley soup combines browned mushrooms, vegetables, herbs, and chewy pearl barley in a deeply savory broth for a comforting one-pot lunch or dinner. Why this mushroom barley soup recipe works

This recipe balances simple preparation with a few small details that make a noticeable difference. Evenly sized ingredients cook at a similar rate, and giving the pan enough room helps food brown rather than steam. Follow the order of the steps and look for the visual cues described in each one.

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin. These measured quantities make 6 servings.

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 450 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 180 g pearl barley, rinsed
  • 1.5 litres vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, optional
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Chopped parsley, to serve

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Warm the oil in a large heavy pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and celery and cook for 5–6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.
  2. Add the mushrooms in a fairly even layer. Cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring now and then, until they release their liquid and begin to brown.
  3. Stir in the garlic and thyme and cook for 30 seconds. Add the rinsed barley, broth, bay leaves, and soy sauce if using.
  4. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer. Cover partially and cook for 35–45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the barley is tender. Add a splash of water or broth if the soup becomes thicker than you like.
  5. Remove the bay leaves. Season with salt and pepper, then rest for 5 minutes. Serve with parsley and crusty bread.

Helpful tips

  • Prep evenly: Cut or portion ingredients to similar sizes so they cook at a consistent pace.
  • Season to taste: Salt levels differ between brands and pantry staples. Start with the listed amount and make small adjustments near the end.
  • Make it your own: Use the optional toppings and suggested swaps to suit what you have available, while keeping the main ingredient ratios close to the recipe.

Storage and make-ahead

Let leftovers cool promptly, transfer them to a clean airtight container, and refrigerate within two hours. For best quality, enjoy within 3 days and reheat only the portion you plan to eat until steaming hot. If the texture changes during storage, add a small splash of water or broth when reheating where appropriate. Do not leave cooked food at room temperature for extended periods.

What to serve with it

Serve this recipe with a simple fresh side, seasonal vegetables, or a grain that complements its flavor. Keep the rest of the meal uncomplicated so the main dish stays the focus.
