Fluffy Classic Pancakes

These fluffy homemade pancakes use simple pantry ingredients and come out tender, golden, and perfect for breakfast or brunch.

Prep: 5 minCook: 15 minServes: 4

The key to light pancakes is mixing the batter only until combined. A few small lumps are fine and help keep the pancakes tender.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 3 tbsp melted butter, plus more for pan
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, optional

Instructions

  1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
  2. Whisk egg, milk, melted butter and vanilla in another bowl.
  3. Pour wet ingredients into dry and stir just until combined.
  4. Heat a lightly buttered skillet over medium heat.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form and edges set, about 2 minutes.
  6. Flip and cook 1–2 minutes more until golden and cooked through.

Tips

  • Rest batter for 5 minutes.
  • Lower heat if pancakes brown too quickly.
  • Freeze cooled pancakes in a single layer for quick breakfasts.

Storage and serving

Serve warm with butter, fruit or syrup. Refrigerate leftovers in a covered container for 3–4 days and reheat thoroughly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use plant milk?

Yes. Choose an unsweetened variety; texture can vary slightly.

Why are my pancakes tough?

Overmixing or overcooking can make them firm. Mix only until the ingredients are combined.

Recipe notes

Cooking times vary with the pan and heat. Adjust sugar and toppings to taste.
