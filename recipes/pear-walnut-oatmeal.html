Pear Walnut Oatmeal

Pear walnut oatmeal is a warm breakfast of creamy oats, ripe pear, toasted walnuts, and cinnamon. It comes together quickly and is easy to adjust with milk or plant-based alternatives.

Prep: 5 minCook: 10 minServes: 2 servings

Why this pear walnut oatmeal recipe works

This recipe balances simple preparation with a few small details that make a noticeable difference. Evenly sized ingredients cook at a similar rate, and giving the pan enough room helps food brown rather than steam. Follow the order of the steps and look for the visual cues described in each one.

Ingredients

Gather everything before you begin. These measured quantities make 2 servings.

  • 100 g rolled oats
  • 400 ml milk or unsweetened plant milk
  • 200 ml water
  • 1 ripe pear, diced
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp chopped walnuts
  • 1–2 tsp maple syrup or honey, optional
  • 1 tbsp yogurt, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Place the oats, milk, water, cinnamon, and salt in a small saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring often.
  2. Lower the heat and cook for 5–7 minutes until the oats are creamy. Add a splash more milk or water if you prefer a looser texture.
  3. Stir in half of the diced pear for the final 2 minutes so it softens slightly while keeping some texture.
  4. Divide the oatmeal between two bowls. Top with the remaining pear and chopped walnuts.
  5. Finish with maple syrup or honey and a spoonful of yogurt if desired. Serve immediately while warm.

Helpful tips

  • Prep evenly: Cut or portion ingredients to similar sizes so they cook at a consistent pace.
  • Season to taste: Salt levels differ between brands and pantry staples. Start with the listed amount and make small adjustments near the end.
  • Make it your own: Use the optional toppings and suggested swaps to suit what you have available, while keeping the main ingredient ratios close to the recipe.

Storage and make-ahead

Let leftovers cool promptly, transfer them to a clean airtight container, and refrigerate within two hours. For best quality, enjoy within 3 days and reheat only the portion you plan to eat until steaming hot. If the texture changes during storage, add a small splash of water or broth when reheating where appropriate. Do not leave cooked food at room temperature for extended periods.
