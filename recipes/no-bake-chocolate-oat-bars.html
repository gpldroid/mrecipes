No-Bake Chocolate Oat Bars

Make no-bake chocolate oat bars with rolled oats, peanut butter, and a smooth chocolate topping for an easy make-ahead snack.

Prep: 15 minCook: 5 minYield: 12 bars

At a glance: 20 minutes · 12 bars · Snack.

These no-bake oat bars set in the refrigerator, so there is no need to turn on the oven. Peanut butter binds the oats, while a thin chocolate layer adds a crisp finish once chilled.

Warm nut butter and syrup coat the oats so the mixture presses together instead of crumbling. Chilling firms the base and lets the chocolate topping slice cleanly.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 180 g rolled oats (about 2 cups)
  • 160 g smooth peanut butter
  • 80 ml maple syrup or honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of fine salt
  • 120 g dark or semi-sweet chocolate, chopped
  • 1 tsp coconut oil or neutral oil, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Line a 20 cm / 8-inch square tin with parchment, leaving overhang on two sides for easy lifting.
  2. Warm peanut butter and maple syrup in a small saucepan over low heat, stirring until smooth. Do not let the mixture boil.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla and salt.
  4. Add rolled oats and mix until every oat is coated. Press firmly and evenly into the lined tin using the back of a spoon or a flat-bottomed cup.
  5. Melt chocolate with the optional oil in short microwave bursts, stirring between each burst until smooth.
  6. Spread the chocolate over the oat base. Tap the tin gently on the counter to level the topping.
  7. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours until firm, then lift out and cut into 12 bars with a sharp knife.

Tips for the best results

  • Press the oat mixture firmly into the tin so the bars hold together when sliced.
  • If the peanut butter is very thick, warm it gently before mixing; avoid overheating it.
  • For a nut-free version, use a seed butter that is suitable for the people eating it.

Serving suggestions

Serve chilled with tea or coffee, or pack a bar for a snack. Let frozen bars thaw in the refrigerator for the best texture.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week. Separate layers with parchment. The bars can be frozen for up to 2 months and thawed in the refrigerator.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use quick oats?

They can work but create a softer, less textured bar. Rolled oats give the best chew.

Why are my bars crumbly?

The binder may not have coated the oats evenly, or the mixture may not have been pressed firmly enough before chilling.
