# MRecipes off-page SEO and editorial outreach

This playbook is for earning relevant editorial mentions and referral links. It does not promise links or search rankings: each publisher decides independently whether to publish, link, and which link attributes to use.

## Editorial standards

- Pitch a genuinely useful cooking story, tested recipe, ingredient guide, or technique—not a request for a link.
- Offer original writing and photographs that MRecipes has the rights to license.
- Never buy links intended to pass ranking signals, exchange links at scale, use automated directory submissions, or require keyword-rich exact-match anchors.
- Disclose any commercial relationship. Paid or sponsored links must use the publisher's appropriate rel="sponsored" or nofollow treatment.
- Prefer a natural brand citation such as “MRecipes” or a descriptive recipe title over repetitive exact-match anchors.
- Track each pitch, response, published URL, destination URL, and link attribute in a campaign sheet. Re-check live links periodically.

## Suggested original pitches

1. **A practical method for writing reliable recipe instructions:** explain ingredient preparation, visual doneness cues, timing ranges, and storage notes, with examples from tested MRecipes recipes.
2. **How to adapt a recipe across measurement systems:** explain grams versus cups, temperature conversion, pan sizes, and why weight is more reliable for baking.
3. **A seasonal recipe story with original photography:** choose a recipe that genuinely fits the publisher's audience and provide complete ingredients, method, yield, preparation time, and cooking time.

Do not submit the same article to multiple publishers as duplicate content. Customize each pitch and article for the target publication.

## Outreach tracking

Record these fields for every prospect:

- Publisher and editorial contact page
- Language and audience fit
- Proposed topic and target MRecipes URL
- Pitch date and follow-up date
- Response and editorial status
- Published article URL
- Whether the MRecipes link is live, relevant, and crawlable
- Link attribute (follow, nofollow, or sponsored)
- Last verification date

## Search Console and Bing follow-through

After an editorial mention is published, verify that the referring page is live and points to the intended canonical MRecipes URL. Use Search Console's Links report and Bing Webmaster Tools' Backlinks report when available. Treat third-party link metrics as estimates, not proof of ranking impact.
