Roasted Chickpea Grain Bowl

Build a colorful roasted chickpea bowl with spiced crispy chickpeas, grains, cucumber, tomatoes, and a lemon-tahini dressing.

Prep: 15 minCook: 30 minYield: 2 servings

At a glance: 45 minutes · 2 servings · Vegetarian Lunch.

This grain bowl combines warm, spiced chickpeas with fresh vegetables and a creamy lemon-tahini dressing. It is easy to assemble from pantry staples and can be adapted to whichever grains or vegetables you have.

Drying chickpeas before roasting helps their surfaces brown, and leaving space on the pan prevents steaming. A dressing made separately lets each component keep its texture until serving.

Ingredients

Measure the ingredients before starting. The amounts below are used in the method and recipe structured data.

  • 1 can (400 g / 14 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 300 g cooked quinoa, brown rice, or bulgur
  • 1/2 cucumber, diced
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 handfuls baby spinach or chopped lettuce
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2–3 tbsp warm water, as needed
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated, optional

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Drain and rinse the chickpeas, then pat them very dry with a clean towel.
  2. Toss chickpeas with olive oil, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, and salt. Spread on a rimmed baking tray in one layer.
  3. Roast for 25–30 minutes, shaking the tray once, until browned and crisp at the edges. Let cool for a few minutes.
  4. While the chickpeas roast, divide the cooked grains between two bowls. Add cucumber, tomatoes, and spinach or lettuce.
  5. Whisk tahini, lemon juice, optional garlic, and 2 tablespoons warm water in a small bowl. Add more water a little at a time until pourable, then season to taste.
  6. Top the bowls with warm chickpeas and drizzle with the lemon-tahini dressing. Serve immediately.

Tips for the best results

  • Dry canned chickpeas thoroughly for better browning, and do not crowd the tray.
  • Keep the dressing separate if packing the bowls for later.
  • Swap grains and vegetables to use what is in season; keep the dressing ratio similar and thin as needed.

Serving suggestions

Add pickled onions, olives, or crumbled feta if desired. For a more filling bowl, increase the grains or add extra greens.

Storage and make-ahead notes

Store chickpeas, grains, vegetables, and dressing separately in covered containers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Re-crisp chickpeas in the oven or air fryer before assembling if possible.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use dried chickpeas?

Yes, but cook them fully first. The roasting step seasons and crisps cooked chickpeas; it does not replace soaking and boiling.

How can I make the dressing thinner?

Whisk in warm water one teaspoon at a time until it pours easily from a spoon.
