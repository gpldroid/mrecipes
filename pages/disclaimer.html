Recipe Disclaimer

General information

Recipes and cooking content are provided for general informational and educational purposes. Results vary between kitchens and ingredients.

Food safety

Use safe food-handling practices, avoid cross-contamination, refrigerate perishables promptly, and follow reliable guidance for safe internal cooking temperatures. Cooking times are estimates, not substitutes for temperature checks.

Allergies and dietary needs

Read ingredient labels and consider cross-contact risks. Readers are responsible for deciding whether a recipe suits their allergies, dietary restrictions, or medical needs. Substitutions can change nutrition and cooking behavior.

Nutrition

Unless a recipe provides a calculated nutrition panel and method, nutritional values should not be assumed. Actual values vary by brand, serving size, and substitutions.
